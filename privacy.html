Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ABR Content Automation handles information when users connect their TikTok accounts and use content publishing workflows.

1. Information We Access

ABR Content Automation may access basic TikTok account information and authorization tokens that are required to connect to TikTok APIs.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized TikTok accounts, manage content publishing workflows, and operate the service.

3. Content Data

Content submitted through the service may be processed for the purpose of preparing, uploading, or publishing content to TikTok.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate the automation workflow and TikTok integration.

5. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect account credentials, access tokens, and service data from unauthorized access.

6. Data Removal

Users may revoke TikTok authorization at any time through their TikTok account settings. Revoking authorization will prevent the service from accessing the account.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact ABR Company.
